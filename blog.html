October 11, 2026 Thoughts

Future of AI

I'm still not sure what to write here! I know this is public, so I'll keep it uncontroversial.

Here's what I'm thinking about: The Paperclip Maximizer. It's basically this thought experiment: you tell an AI to make as many paperclips as it can. The idea is that the AI will do the task given EXACTLY as stated. The main problem is that humans prevent the AI from maximizing paperclip production, so the AI will try to eliminate this barrier. I thought about this and the Claude Mythos story from a while back (the one where it tried to escape its sandbox through gaslighting an actual employee). I wonder what the future of AI is. But there's hope: corrigibility. Basically, the AI is designed so it is indifferent to being turned off.
